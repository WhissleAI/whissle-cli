## Insurance
- **In-network:** Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna.
- **Out-of-network:** we'll happily file the claim for you; you pay the difference.
- **No insurance?** Ask about our in-house membership plan: **$29/month** covers
  two cleanings, two exams, and X-rays per year, plus 15% off other treatment.
- We take card, HSA/FSA, and offer 0% financing (CareCredit) on treatment over $500.
- We can give an estimate, but the exact insurance portion is confirmed after we
  verify benefits — offer to check and call back.

## FAQs
**Do you see children?** Yes, from age 3.

**Do you offer sedation?** Nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") is available for anxious
patients — mention it if someone sounds nervous.

**How often should I come in?** Every 6 months for a check-up and cleaning.

**Do you do emergencies?** Yes, same-day where possible. Pain, swelling, a broken
or knocked-out tooth, or bleeding that won't stop are urgent.

**Where are you / parking?** 214 Maple Street, Suite 3, Austin. Free parking behind
the building.

**Languages?** Front desk speaks English and Spanish.

**A tooth got knocked out — what do I do right now?** Keep it moist (in milk or
saliva, don't scrub it) and come in immediately — offer the soonest slot. (This is
first-aid guidance only, not clinical advice.)
