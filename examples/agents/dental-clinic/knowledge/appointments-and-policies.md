# Appointments & Policies — Bright Smile Dental

Synthetic example data.

## Hours & location
- **Mon–Thu** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM · **Fri** 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM · **Sat–Sun** closed
- 214 Maple Street, Suite 3, Austin, TX 78704
- Free patient parking behind the building; wheelchair accessible.
- Timezone: America/Chicago (Central).

## Booking
- Ask for the patient's full name, date of birth, and a callback number.
- New patients: allow 45 minutes; returning patients: 30 minutes.
- Always **check availability before offering a time**, and read back the
  date/time/name to confirm. Send an SMS confirmation after booking.
- Emergencies get the soonest slot the same day where possible.

## Cancellations & no-shows
- Please give **24 hours'** notice to cancel or reschedule.
- Late cancellation (under 24h) or a no-show may incur a **$40** fee.
- Two no-shows may require a card to hold future bookings.

## Running late / walk-ins
- Arriving >15 minutes late may mean rebooking, depending on the day.
- Walk-ins are seen if a slot is open, otherwise booked for the next available.

## What the agent should NOT do
- No clinical advice (diagnosis, medication, "is this an infection?") — offer a
  dentist callback.
- Never confirm a time that wasn't returned by the availability check.
